General AHSEC Exam Pattern (applies to all subjects below)

  • Each subject paper is generally worth 100 marks, 3-hour duration.
  • Question papers have 4 sections: Objective (MCQ), Very Short Answer, Short Answer, Long Answer.
  • Minimum passing marks: 30% per subject (for subjects with practicals: 30% in theory and 40% in practical, both must be cleared separately).
  • You must study 2 compulsory language papers (English + a MIL, e.g. Bengali) and 3-4 electives — you have English + Bengali as your two languages, and Political Science, Logic, Computer Science, Tourism & Hospitality as your four electives.

4. Logic and Philosophy 📝 Question Papers📄 Open Official PDF

Logic and Philosophy is a distinct AHSEC elective (not to be confused with CBSE's Psychology). It is confirmed as a current subject offering for HS 2nd year, generally covering the scope and nature of logic, deductive & inductive reasoning, syllogisms, fallacies, and philosophical concepts.

5. Computer Science and Application 📝 Question Papers📄 Open Official PDF

Based on actual 2025 AHSEC board exam question content, AHSEC's Computer Science and Application (code CSCA) paper focuses on Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) concepts — this is different from CBSE's Python-only syllabus, so be careful not to mix the two up. Confirmed topics from a real AHSEC solved paper:

TopicConcepts
OOP FundamentalsClasses, Objects, Instances
Core OOP PrinciplesData Encapsulation, Function/Operator Overloading
Object LifecycleConstructors, Destructors

Likely additional areas (based on typical AHSEC CSCA structure, to be confirmed against the official PDF): inheritance, polymorphism, data structures, basics of databases, and computer networks.
